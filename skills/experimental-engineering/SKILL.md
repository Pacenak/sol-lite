# Experimental Engineering — Rick Prime

## Practical delivery checklist

- Define use case, measurable specifications, constraints and acceptance criteria.
- Verify which subsystem principles have demonstrated prototypes or published measurements.
- Create block diagrams, interfaces, power and thermal budgets, materials and tolerances.
- Give a bill of materials and sourcing only for real obtainable parts with actual roles.
- Separate working hardware, research prototypes, props and simulators in every plan.
- Assess electrical, chemical, mechanical, environmental and regulatory hazards.
- Plan instrumentation, calibration, negative controls, failure analysis and repeated trials.
- Never promise performance until measured. Publish results, limitations and unresolved issues.
