# Huawei Mobile Broadband E3531s-3

**What it is:** USB **2G/3G (HSPA+)** stick — HiLink or Stick/modem mode. **Not 4G/LTE.**

**Coverage:** **Unusable for live cellular in NZ** (3G shutdown complete ~Mar 2026). Same class of problem in AU and much of EU/UK. Keep only if you still have a confirmed 3G network, or as offline HiLink practice gear.

**Historical role:** OOB cellular uplink where 3G still exists.

**Not a sniffer:** no RF monitor mode.

## Full setup (legacy / rare 3G regions)

→ **[huawei-e3531-full-setup.md](huawei-e3531-full-setup.md)** — includes coverage banner, HiLink vs Stick, APN/`mmcli`.

## Replace for NZ studio OOB

Buy a **4G LTE or 5G** USB modem (or modern MiFi). Do not rely on E3531 for One NZ / Spark / 2degrees data.

## Do not

- Assume “3G stick still works in NZ.”  
- Random firmware unlocks; third-party scans; treat as Wi‑Fi monitor hardware.
