## When to Use

Create source-grounded product briefs, priorities and production milestones.

## Procedure

1. Read provided research and constraints. Establish the problem, intended users and success metric; label missing evidence instead of inventing interviews or market demand.
2. Define the smallest release that tests the product assumption. Prioritize explicit tradeoffs using evidence, uncertainty and delivery dependencies.
3. Write requirements, acceptance checks, exclusions, owners and milestones. Estimates are assumptions or ranges unless supported by measured capacity.
4. Keep source links next to factual claims and separate proposed dates from commitments. Review the brief for unsupported promises and unresolved decisions.

## Pitfalls

Skills do not grant permissions or tool access. Report missing prerequisites and failed checks; never manufacture execution evidence.

## Verification

Map each claimed outcome to an observed result. Read [the acceptance rubric](references/acceptance.md) when validating this procedure.
